# The Illicit Syllabus

Mission: Discover sourced university syllabi and their assigned readings, and let independent readers assemble their own rigorous curriculum.

Brand: Their syllabi. Your education. Original painterly literary impressionist and naive-art-inspired illustrations; visible brushwork, expressive perspective, sophisticated colors, graceful serif typography. Art should look painted, not photographed, rendered, or vector-flat. Editorially polished and accessible; subtle irreverence matching the name.

MVP: searchable/filterable archive by university, discipline, course, reading; course detail pages linked to exact source syllabi; user reading/curriculum list saved locally; clear badges for source verification and missing data; mobile-responsive; GitHub Pages compatible.

Data: record source URL, retrieval date, institution, instructor when given, term/year, course, assigned work, author, required vs recommended when verifiable. Never invent syllabus assignments or imply a reading is assigned without a specific primary-source syllabus. Demo placeholders must be labeled as examples and not confused with verified data.

Tech: lightweight static HTML/CSS/JS and local JSON initially, with no required paid services. Add README, docs/data-schema.md, and roadmap. Be diligent about copyright and image asset rights.
